TappyPlane Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Welcome to TappyPlane. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

TappyPlane itself does not directly collect personally identifiable information. However, third-party services used in the game may automatically collect certain data, including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Country and language settings
  • Gameplay activity
  • Crash reports and diagnostics

2. Advertising

TappyPlane displays third-party advertisements to support the game.

We may use advertising services such as:

  • Google AdMob

These providers may use cookies, advertising identifiers, and similar technologies to display personalized or non-personalized ads.

Learn more about how Google uses information:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We may use third-party analytics or crash reporting tools to improve gameplay experience and application stability.

These services may collect:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Gameplay session information
  • Crash logs

4. Children’s Privacy

TappyPlane is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove the data.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information and work with trusted third-party providers. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

The game may contain integrations or links to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

Example:

  • Google Privacy Policy

7. Your Choices

Depending on your device and region, you may:

  • Reset your advertising ID
  • Limit ad personalization
  • Disable personalized ads through device settings

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
